About School

HISTORY

Max Arthur Macauliffe Public School, situated in Samrala, Ludhiana, Punjab was established in 2000 and officially opened its doors on March 2001. It is a co-educational institution affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education and is managed by S. Jagir Singh Charitable Trust and is named after Max Arthur Macauliffe, a renowned English translator of Sikh Scriptures and historian of early Sikhism. Sant Tirath Singh Ji Sewa Panthi head of Bhai Kanahiya Sect laid foundation of this Institute on 10th March, 2000, where Sant Ji spoke only about Macauliffe and the education standard of the western world.

Initially the school was started with just 6 rooms, having 18 students and Admin Block. Despite the limited resources, the school’s founders were driven by a passion for providing quality education and promoting values. The school got affiliated to the CBSE in year 2013 upto class 10 and in year 2015 for class XI & XII.

S. Davinder Singh s/o S. Jagir Singh articulated the school’s mission to provide quality education that integrates modern pedagogical methods with a rich cultural and spiritual heritage of Sikhism.

By adopting Macauliffe’s name the school aims to embody Macauliffe’s commitment to education, Cultural understanding and the dissemination of Sikh philosophy. It serves as a tribute to his efforts in bringing Sikhism to the attention of the western world.

OUR EDUCATION SYSTEM

M. A. M. Public School is affiliated with “Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE),” a widely recognized and highly regarded school board in the country. We provide an effective framework for educational balanced, rigorously-taught and monitored curriculum.

As students advance through the school, we ensure that their needs are met across subject and year groups, through progressive and continuous assessment and review.
