Dear Parents Greetings from Max Arthur Macauliffe Public School, Samrala! Thank you for choosing Max Arthur Macauliffe Public School for your children.

School is committed to give its pupils a strong foundation in a nurturing environment. One that will allow their natural gifts to unfold and make learning pleasurable.

School is always a place where one receives training for life, where effective, meaningful and joyful learning takes place.
